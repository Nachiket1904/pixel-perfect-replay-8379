## Why this matters
Supervised learning (learning from labelled examples) covers most real ML work: predicting prices, classifying emails, scoring risk.

## What to learn
- Regression vs classification
- Linear and logistic regression: what the model actually learns
- Decision trees, random forests and gradient boosting
- The scikit-learn pattern: `fit`, `predict`, `score`

## Try this
On one classification dataset, train logistic regression, a decision tree and a random forest. Compare their test accuracy, then explain in two sentences why the best one might be winning.

## Tip
Always start with a simple baseline (even "predict the most common class") so you know whether your model is actually learning anything.
