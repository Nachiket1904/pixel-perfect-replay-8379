## Why this matters
Tutorials teach tools one at a time. A small project forces you to combine them, and it's the first thing you can show in a portfolio or interview.

## The task
Pick a small public dataset (a Kaggle CSV is fine) and answer three questions about it in one Jupyter notebook.

1. Load it with Pandas and describe it: rows, columns, missing values.
2. Clean it: fix types, handle missing values, drop obvious junk.
3. Answer your three questions with `groupby`, filters and NumPy.
4. Make two or three Matplotlib charts that support your answers.
5. Write a short summary at the top: what you found and what surprised you.

## Done when
Someone else can run your notebook top to bottom without errors and understand your conclusions from the summary alone. Push it to GitHub with a README.
