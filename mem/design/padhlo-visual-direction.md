---
name: PadhLo visual direction
description: Color, typography, and tone requirements for the PadhLo With Us learning platform.
type: design
---

- Use light mode, clean card-based layouts, generous whitespace, and subtle shadows; avoid heavy gradients.
- Primary deep indigo: #4F46E5. Accent amber: #F59E0B for progress, calls to action, and highlights. Use slate neutrals, green for done, and red for errors.
- Use Inter for UI and JetBrains Mono for code.
- Tone: friendly senior developer; clear and encouraging, never hype-driven.
- Do not invent statistics, user counts, testimonials, or company logos.
